abstract: >
    Research data often come in groups: students within schools, patients measured over several visits, or participants responding to many items. Such observations are not independent, and ordinary regression can give misleading results. This chapter introduces mixed-effects models with lme4, covering random intercepts and slopes, interpreting and reporting results, and extensions to binary and count outcomes, culminating in a case study of repeated measurements from a longitudinal study.
